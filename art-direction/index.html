How a SIDE EYE issue gets designed

Everything a new art director, vendor or AI agent needs to design a SIDE EYE issue, from the brief to print-ready files for Amazon KDP. Start with document 00. Brand rules (logo, color, voice) live in the Brand Standards hub, and the hub wins if the two ever disagree.

The editable master copies are Google Docs in the SIDE EYE Drive, in Side Eye › Side Eye Art Direction › Magazine. Ask the art director for access.

Some rules say “until Heather decides.” Follow that rule as written. It is the rule until a new decision lands in the Decision Log.

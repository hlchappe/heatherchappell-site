**Who this is for:** Every designer, art director, editor, vendor and AI agent working on a SIDE EYE issue, and anyone who signs off a stage.

## How to use these checklists

1. The stages match 01 Issue Design Process: **0 Issue Brief, 1 Flatplan, 2 Art Direction, 3 Art Production, 4 Layout, 5 Review and Legal Read, 6 Prepress, 7 Proof Copy, 8 Release and Archive.**
2. Every item is a yes or no. Tick it only if it's true now, for every page or image it covers.
3. **Any "no" stops the gate.** Fix it, or write it down as an exception with the reason and get Heather's yes for that exception. An exception never covers a hard rule (CMYK, 240% ink, 300 ppi, rights cleared, no generated lettering).
4. The "Who" column says who checks. The person who made the work never signs off their own work alone. Where both a maker and a checker are listed, both tick.
5. Copy the checklist into the issue's working folder (see 09 File Naming, Folders and Handoff Specs), fill it in, and keep it with the stage's files. A gate isn't passed until the filled checklist is saved.
6. **Only Heather approves a gate.** AI agents never mark work Approved or Published, and never send, post, upload, order or buy anything on their own (hub, "What always needs Heather's yes").
7. When a rule here and a source doc disagree, the Brand Standards hub's Decision log wins, then the newest Heather decision. Report the clash in the issue's open items; don't decide it yourself.

### Who checks: roles

These are the role names from 01 Issue Design Process: From Brief to Print-Ready Files, section 2.

| Role | Checks |
|---|---|
| Heather (Publisher / Editor in Chief) | Approves every gate, every spend, the upload and the release |
| Art Director (AD) | The brief draft, flatplan, art-direction docs, every image and the look of every spread |
| Designer / Layout | Pages, cover, captions and credits as built |
| Illustrator / Image Generator | Prompts, selects, upscales, edits and the Prompt Log |
| Rights Checker | Every image, font and reproduced text cleared and credited; the Rights Log |
| Fact-check / Research | Facts, proof levels, display text, the Must NOT list and the legal read checklist |
| Production | Color, files, preflight, KDP previewer, proof checks and the archive package |

One person may hold more than one role, but nobody checks their own work. Where two roles are listed on a line, both tick. Sensitivity and expert reads are run by the readers the Issue Brief names.

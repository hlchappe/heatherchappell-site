Quality Checklists

Version 1.1 draft · November 25, 2026 · Owner: Gus Pressley, Art Director, Production and Prepress

Status: DRAFT for Heather's review

Midjourney rules follow the Brand Standards hub, section 11 (Midjourney), as of November 25, 2026. Where this document and section 11 differ, section 11 wins.

Who this is for: Every designer, art director, editor, vendor and AI agent working on a SIDE EYE issue, and anyone who signs off a stage.

Use it when: You finish a stage of the issue and need to prove it's done before the next stage starts. Each checklist is a gate.

How to use these checklists

  1. The stages match 01 Issue Design Process: 0 Issue Brief, 1 Flatplan, 2 Art Direction, 3 Art Production, 4 Layout, 5 Review and Legal Read, 6 Prepress, 7 Proof Copy, 8 Release and Archive.
  2. Every item is a yes or no. Tick it only if it's true now, for every page or image it covers.
  3. Any "no" stops the gate. Fix it, or write it down as an exception with the reason and get Heather's yes for that exception. An exception never covers a hard rule (CMYK, 240% ink, 300 ppi, rights cleared, no generated lettering).
  4. The "Who" column says who checks. The person who made the work never signs off their own work alone. Where both a maker and a checker are listed, both tick.
  5. Copy the checklist into the issue's working folder (see 09 File Naming, Folders and Handoff Specs), fill it in, and keep it with the stage's files. A gate isn't passed until the filled checklist is saved.
  6. Only Heather approves a gate. AI agents never mark work Approved or Published, and never send, post, upload, order or buy anything on their own (hub, "What always needs Heather's yes").
  7. When a rule here and a source doc disagree, the Brand Standards hub's Decision log wins, then the newest Heather decision. Report the clash in the issue's open items; don't decide it yourself.

Who checks: roles

These are the role names from 01 Issue Design Process: From Brief to Print-Ready Files, section 2.

Role Checks
Heather (Publisher / Editor in Chief) Approves every gate, every spend, the upload and the release
Art Director (AD) The brief draft, flatplan, art-direction docs, every image and the look of every spread
Designer / Layout Pages, cover, captions and credits as built
Illustrator / Image Generator Prompts, selects, upscales, edits and the Prompt Log
Rights Checker Every image, font and reproduced text cleared and credited; the Rights Log
Fact-check / Research Facts, proof levels, display text, the Must NOT list and the legal read checklist
Production Color, files, preflight, KDP previewer, proof checks and the archive package

One person may hold more than one role, but nobody checks their own work. Where two roles are listed on a line, both tick. Sensitivity and expert reads are run by the readers the Issue Brief names.

Stage 2: Art Direction doc review

The art-direction doc follows the model in 05 Art and Illustration Direction (with Midjourney Prompt Standards) and the art-direction doc template in 11 Templates.

# Check Who Yes
2.1 Every page has a concept: one visual idea tied to the article's thesis in a sentence, with the joke named AD [ ]
2.2 Every page says what pink marks (one thing per page in print) AD [ ]
2.3 The print style table lists process, inks, paper ground, field color and type faces for every image AD [ ]
2.4 Each image has exactly one process: RELIEF, COLLAGE, WOOD TYPE, HYBRID or NEWSPRINT. RELIEF and COLLAGE mix only by the HYBRID recipe AD [ ]
2.5 HYBRID is used only for openers about a real historical person AD [ ]
2.6 Each image uses black plus two inks at most (covers and the tabloid poster may use more) AD [ ]
2.7 QUIET pages plan no neon, no collage, no jokes and no pink AD, Fact-check / Research [ ]
2.8 Every generated image has three prompts, A (safe read), B (bold joke) and C (a different take), each complete and copy-ready in its own code block, with no placeholders AD [ ]
2.9 Every prompt uses --raw (never --style raw) and --v 8.2, with --hd on full-page, spread and cover art. Stylize: photos --s 50 to 100, prints and illustration --s 100 to 250, never above 300. Chaos 0 for finals. Personalization, --weird, --exp, --q, --sv, --oref, --ow, --cref and :: weights are off AD [ ]
2.9a Every prompt has a --no list with at least text, letters, words, numbers, logo, watermark (prints add glow, gradient, chrome) AD [ ]
2.9b No prompt uses gloss words (photorealistic, cinematic, 8k, masterpiece, ultra realistic, glowing, epic). Photo prompts name the real camera, film, light and two or three imperfections; photographic stills carry no neon or pink AD [ ]
2.10 Style references are only SIDE EYE's own approved images, at the weights in 05; never --sw without --sref; no moodboards; source image URLs go in the prompt (Drive links don't work) AD [ ]
2.11 No prompt names an artist, studio, shop or magazine AD (second reviewer) [ ]
2.12 No prompt asks for text; paper, ledgers, letters and signs are described as "completely blank" AD [ ]
2.13 Every historical image is listed with artist, title, date, holding institution, license or public-domain line and source Rights Checker [ ]
2.14 The "Built in layout" table lists everything made by hand or in layout: lettering, wood-type plates, cover lines, captions, composites, ornaments AD [ ]
2.15 Every image has a credit line in the house format Rights Checker [ ]
2.16 The doc was checked against each article's Scorecard "Must NOT claim" list Fact-check / Research [ ]
2.17 Neon builds and any new tint are flagged for the proof Production [ ]
2.18 For covers and openers about living people, an independent reviewer checked the plan AD (independent) [ ]
2.19 Heather has approved the art-direction doc Heather [ ]

Stage 3: Art Production

3A. Per-image check

Run this on every finished image before it goes into layout. It expands the checklist in the art-direction skill and applies the hub's newer decisions.

# Check Who Yes
3A.1 The image uses the process the art-direction doc assigned, and only that one AD [ ]
3A.2 Black plus two inks at most (covers and the tabloid poster may use more) AD [ ]
3A.3 Pink marks one thing in the image. If two things are pink, one is wrong AD [ ]
3A.4 The joke is true on the evidence: the art implies no crime, deal or motive that the article's Scorecard rates below its top proof level Fact-check / Research [ ]
3A.5 Living people appear only as caricature or print, never photoreal, and only doing what sourced public facts support Fact-check / Research [ ]
3A.6 Historical figures, if shown, appear as caricature, real period art or a generated likeness (photo-real period stills included); all are allowed AD [ ]
3A.7 Letters to the AI Dead figures are skulls wearing one defining prop, never a portrait AD [ ]
3A.8 The image contains no generated lettering, letters or fake writing anywhere, including on signs, papers and labels AD [ ]
3A.9 Period art is not recolored pink or neon; peach is used only on cut-out skin AD [ ]
3A.10 The illustration does not imitate a period document and does not sit inside one AD [ ]
3A.11 Neon reads as flat ink on paper: no glow, chrome, gradient or screen sheen AD [ ]
3A.12 The image looks printed, pasted or carved by hand (paper ground, halftone, speckle), not rendered on a screen AD [ ]
3A.13 Halftone is coarse and applied at final size; no two halftone layers overlap Production [ ]
3A.14 QUIET-page images are NEWSPRINT only: no neon, no collage, no joke AD [ ]
3A.15 No readable brand logos or personal information appear in found material Rights Checker [ ]
3A.16 The final prompt (A or B), settings and style reference are recorded in the art log AD [ ]
3A.17 Effective resolution at placed size is 300 ppi or more (600 for line art and scans), never under 200 Production [ ]
3A.18 The print file is CMYK or Grayscale, with flat-ink art rebuilt to the house builds by number Production [ ]
3A.19 Total ink is 240% or less across the image Production [ ]
3A.20 The RGB master and the print file are both saved and named per 09 Designer / Layout [ ]
3A.21 Neon builds in the image are flagged for the proof Production [ ]

If any image will also be used in video, it also passes the pause test: no frame could pass for real footage of a real person (hub, AI-generated video).

Stage 4: Layout

4A. Per-spread layout check

Run on every spread. The rules come from 03 Page and Spread Standards and 04 Grid and Typography.

# Check Who Yes
4A.1 Page size is 8.625 x 11.25 in with 0.125 in bleed on top, bottom and outside Designer / Layout [ ]
4A.2 Text sits inside the house margins (outside 0.5, top 0.5, bottom 0.625, gutter 0.375 in) Designer / Layout [ ]
4A.3 No text or essential art is closer than 0.375 in to the trim Production [ ]
4A.4 Faces, headline letters and box-score figures stay 0.5 in clear of the gutter Designer / Layout [ ]
4A.5 Images crossing the gutter have an unimportant middle and 0.25 in duplicated on each side Designer / Layout [ ]
4A.6 Layout is built on the 12-column grid; body, captions and sidebars sit on the 13 pt baseline Designer / Layout [ ]
4A.7 LOUD body is 3 columns, Tiempos Text (or its stand-in) 9.5/13; QUIET body is 2 columns, 10/15 Designer / Layout [ ]
4A.8 Headline 60 to 96 pt, deck 16/20, caption 7.5/10 mono, sidebar 8.5/12, folio 7 pt mono caps Designer / Layout [ ]
4A.9 Nothing is smaller than 7 pt; no line is thinner than 0.75 pt Production [ ]
4A.10 Body text is horizontal and on a clean paper panel Designer / Layout [ ]
4A.11 Headlines run on other axes only on LOUD openers, with at most one diagonal per spread AD [ ]
4A.12 Every page has one pink focus at most AD [ ]
4A.13 QUIET pages have no neon, no collage, no jokes and no pink: black, gray and one muted tab tint AD, Fact-check / Research [ ]
4A.14 The filing tab, folio and corner bar are correct for the section Designer / Layout [ ]
4A.15 Features carry the fixed frame: headline and subhead, opening, body, twist, What We Can Prove / What We Can't box, the other side, why the gap matters, sources line Fact-check / Research [ ]
4A.16 Every image has its caption and credit line in place Designer / Layout, Rights Checker [ ]
4A.17 Money pages are readable in under 15 seconds, one point per infographic, no block over 120 words Fact-check / Research, AD [ ]
4A.18 Classifieds are 7 pt mono in two or three narrow columns, with no images Designer / Layout [ ]
4A.19 No generated lettering anywhere; every word is set in type or hand-lettered AD [ ]
4A.20 All swatches are house CMYK builds by number; no RGB, spot or Lab swatches Production [ ]
4A.21 Small text is K100 and set to overprint Production [ ]
4A.22 No overset text, no empty frames, no placeholder boxes or placeholder copy Designer / Layout [ ]
4A.23 No font outside the house list appears (no fallback fonts) Production [ ]
4A.24 Logo, where used, is on an approved background with at least one iris width of clear space AD [ ]

Stage 5: Review and Legal Read (copy and legal handoff)

The legal read comes last, after layout, so it reads what will print. Use this check to hand the laid-out issue to the editor and standards reader, and to hand it back to layout.

# Check Who Yes
5.1 A full low-resolution review PDF of the current layout was sent for the read, with its date and build in the file name Designer / Layout [ ]
5.2 Every claim in the issue has a source and a proof level in the fact-check log Fact-check / Research [ ]
5.3 The back-of-issue sources section matches the fact-check log one to one, with page numbers Fact-check / Research [ ]
5.4 Headlines, decks, cover lines, captions, pull quotes and jokes claim no more than the proof supports Fact-check / Research [ ]
5.5 The legal read checklist was run by someone who did not write the piece. Every line or image that frames a living person as doing wrong is softened to court and filing language, or cut. (Heather's decision, November 24, 2026) Fact-check / Research [ ]
5.6 The Must NOT list was checked line by line against the text and the art (the art binds as much as the text) Fact-check / Research, AD [ ]
5.7 Any sensitivity or expert reads the brief calls for are done; no joke punches down; QUIET subjects are treated as QUIET Readers named in the brief, AD [ ]
5.8 No banned words or phrases, including "receipts" used as a metaphor for proof Fact-check / Research [ ]
5.9 The corrections page prints every known error in the same size type as the original error Fact-check / Research [ ]
5.10 The colophon carries the Midjourney credit, all image credits and the influence credits Fact-check / Research, Rights Checker [ ]
5.11 Hosts appear by first name only, with no last names, phone numbers, email or home location anywhere in the issue Fact-check / Research [ ]
5.12 Heather's notes and the read's fixes are sorted into fixes and Change Requests, by page number, in one list AD [ ]
5.13 After fixes, each changed page was checked again; no text changed after the read without a re-read of that page Fact-check / Research, AD [ ]
5.14 Heather has signed off text and layout for prepress. After this, changes need a Change Request Heather [ ]

Open items to watch here (see 13 Open Questions for Heather): whether the no-em-dash rule covers print design copy, and the name of the back-of-issue sources section.

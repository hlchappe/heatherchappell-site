## Notes
- Name queries win on entity clarity: the same one-line identity ("writer, co-host of SIDE EYE, B2B demand generation and growth marketing leader") appears in the hero intro, home meta description, and should match Person schema and LinkedIn/YouTube bios (sameAs).
- Episode/article topic queries are competitive (Wikipedia, Smithsonian). Our angle is the specific twist (newspapers and Murdoch, Napoleon's backers, patent renewals), so keep those words in titles and H1s.
- Deep Dive queries are time-sensitive; titles name the entity (Nvidia, NCIC, Iran) first.
